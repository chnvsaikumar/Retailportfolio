Common questions

How do I buy a channel?

Pick a listing, request the price, review the analytics, and pay into escrow. After transfer is confirmed, the payment is released to the seller.

What happens if the transfer fails?

You get a full refund. Money is never released to the seller until you have access.

How are prices decided?

Sellers set a price based on subscribers, views, niche and revenue. Monetized channels usually cost more than non-monetized ones.

How do I sell my channel?

Use “Sell a channel”, share your channel details, and we review the listing before it goes live.
