FAQ

Quick answers before we talk

Who do you work with?

Fashion, footwear and lifestyle retailers, D2C brands and online sellers who want clearer inventory, buying and sales decisions.

How does a project start?

Send me a message on WhatsApp or email with your goal and a sample of your sales and stock data. I’ll reply with how I’d approach it.

Do you only do planning work?

No. Alongside planning, I offer design, website development, digital, influencer and political marketing, and ecommerce store setup.

Which tools do you use?

Excel, Power Query and Power Pivot for most planning work, and I’m familiar with Oracle RMS and SAP S/4HANA environments.
